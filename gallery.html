About the works ~ notes from the sketchbook

When I am not writing satellite telemetry code or debugging STM32 drivers, I paint. This collection is a tactile counterbalance to embedded systems: wet gouache gradients, pressed terrace bougainvillea, analog contact prints, and pastel spectrograms. Every piece was made with paint, paper, and a stubborn desire to slow down.
